Building a Healthy Plate

Why Is Healthy Food Important?

Eating healthy food helps us get energy for school, sports and other activities. We should try to eat different fruits, vegetables, grains and protein foods.

Learn more: MyPlate Nutrition Guide

Choosing Healthy Snacks

Snacks help us stay energised between meals. Some good choices are fruits, nuts, yoghurt and homemade snacks. We can also enjoy other foods as part of a balanced diet.

Read more: WHO Healthy Diet Guide

Nutrition Tips for Students

Drink More Water

Water helps our body work properly and keeps us hydrated. Carrying a water bottle to school is a simple way to remember to drink.

Eat Different Colours

Fruits and vegetables come in many colours and provide different nutrients. Try adding different colours to your meals to enjoy a variety of foods.

Find more information: Nutrition.gov

Quick Nutrition Note

Try to include vegetables, fruits, grains and protein in your meals. Eating a variety of foods helps your body get different nutrients.
